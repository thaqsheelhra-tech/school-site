FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions, answered.

How long does onboarding take?

Typically 7-14 days. Our team handles data migration from your existing system, sets up classes and rosters, trains your faculty (2-3 sessions), and launches the parent app via WhatsApp invites.

Will my data be safe?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We're hosted on AWS Mumbai region, fully compliant with India's DPDP Act 2023. You own your data — export anytime.

What if our school has bus drivers who aren't tech-savvy?

The driver app is purposefully simple: one big "Start Route" button, a list of stops, and student names. No typing, no menus. Most drivers learn it in under 5 minutes.

Can parents without smartphones use Scholaria?

Yes. Parents can opt-in to SMS notifications for attendance, fees, and emergencies. Critical updates work without the app.

Do you support Hindi and regional languages?

The app currently supports English and Hindi. Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, and Marathi are on our 2026 roadmap.

What payment gateway is used for fees?

We integrate with Razorpay (default) and PayU. Schools can also opt for direct bank transfer with UPI verification.

Can we cancel anytime?

Yes. No long-term contracts. Pay monthly or yearly. If you cancel, you get a full data export.
