PART ONE

Getting Started

Welcome to Scholaria. Whether you're a student excited about your new school app, a teacher looking to save hours every week, or a parent wanting peace of mind, this guide will get you up and running in under 10 minutes.

Before you start, you'll need:
  • A smartphone (Android 8.0+ or iPhone 12+)
  • The invitation SMS or email from your school
  • An internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data)

Installation

01

Find Scholaria in your app store

Open the Google Play Store (Android) or App Store (iPhone). Search for "Scholaria" — look for the gold "S" logo on a navy background.

02

Tap Install

The app is approximately 40 MB. Installation takes 30 seconds on Wi-Fi. You won't be charged — Scholaria is paid for by your school.

03

Open the app

You'll see the Scholaria splash screen (a gold "S" on a dark blue background) followed by the role selection page.

💡
Tip: If your school sent you a direct link in SMS, tap it — it will take you straight to the app store with Scholaria pre-loaded.

Your First Login

When you open Scholaria for the first time, you'll see five role cards. Tap the one that applies to you:

RoleFor whomIdentifying credential
🎓 StudentChildren enrolled in the schoolRoll number + DOB
📚 TeacherFaculty membersFaculty ID + password
🛡️ Head of TeachersPrincipals & vice-principalsAdmin ID + 2FA
❤️ ParentParents/guardiansPhone + OTP
🚌 Bus DriverBus drivers & conductorsBus ID + PIN

Logging in

  1. Tap your role card.
  2. Enter the credentials shared by your school office in your welcome SMS.
  3. Tap "Enter as [Role]".
  4. For parent/student first login: you'll receive an OTP on your registered mobile.
  5. Create a 4-digit PIN for future quick access.
⚠️ Important: Never share your login credentials with anyone — not even other family members. Each parent should have their own account.

FOR STUDENTS

Student Guide

Your home base for everything school. Five tabs at the bottom of the screen take you everywhere you need.

The Five Tabs

🏠 Dashboard

Today's classes, attendance %, pending homework, and announcements.

📅 Timetable

Your full weekly schedule, color-coded by subject.

📝 Homework

All assignments with due dates. Tap to submit.

🏅 Results

Exam scores, grades, ranks, and performance trends.

💬 Messages

Chat directly with your teachers.

Checking your daily classes

  1. Open the app — you land on the Dashboard.
  2. Scroll to "Today's Classes".
  3. The class happening right now is highlighted in gold.
  4. Tap any class to see notes uploaded by that teacher.

Submitting homework

  1. Go to the Homework tab.
  2. Find your assignment. Red badge = high priority.
  3. Tap to open it. Read the description.
  4. Tap Submit → choose how to upload (photo, PDF, or typed answer).
  5. You'll see a green ✓ confirmation. Your teacher gets notified instantly.

Viewing exam results

Go to Results tab. You'll see:

  • Overall percentage and class rank at the top
  • Subject-wise breakdown with grades
  • Progress bars showing how you did vs maximum
  • Trend chart showing improvement over terms
💡
Tip: Long-press a subject to see your teacher's detailed feedback and suggestions for improvement.

FOR TEACHERS

Teacher Guide

Designed to save you 30+ minutes a day. Mark attendance in 30 seconds, assign homework in 60, grade papers in your pocket.

Marking attendance

  1. Tap the Attendance tab.
  2. Select your class from the dropdown.
  3. You'll see a grid of student names — all marked Present (green) by default.
  4. Tap any student to mark them Absent (turns red) or Late (turns yellow).
  5. Tap Save Attendance. Parents of absent students get an SMS within 60 seconds.

Assigning homework

  1. Go to Homework tab → tap + Assign New.
  2. Choose: subject, class, due date, priority (Low/Medium/High).
  3. Type the assignment description.
  4. Optional: attach a PDF, image, or link.
  5. Tap Publish. Every student in that class gets an instant notification.

Grading submissions

  1. Open Homework → tap an assignment.
  2. Tap Submissions ({n}) to see who's submitted.
  3. Open each student's work. Annotate with the pencil tool if needed.
  4. Enter a score (or grade A+/A/B+/B/C/D), and a comment.
  5. Tap Save & Next to move to the next submission.

Broadcasting circulars

Need to send a class-wide message? Go to Circulars → + New. Choose recipients (whole class, specific section, or all parents). Set category and urgency. Hit publish.

FOR HEAD OF TEACHERS

Head of Teachers Guide

Your bird's-eye view of the entire school. From attendance trends to admissions to faculty performance — it all lives here.

Daily morning ritual

Open the app at 9:00 AM. The dashboard banner shows your at-a-glance picture: total students present today, school-wide attendance %, applications pending review, and fee collection status.

Reviewing admission applications

  1. Tap Admissions in the menu (web admin only — coming to mobile soon).
  2. Filter by status: New, Documents Pending, Interview Scheduled, Accepted, Rejected.
  3. Tap an applicant to see their full profile, test scores, and documents.
  4. Add interview notes. Make a decision: Accept, Reject, or Request More Info.

Reading school analytics

The Analytics tab shows:

  • Average performance by grade — spot which grades need intervention
  • Yearly enrollment trend — growth/decline over 7 years
  • Subject performance heatmap — which subjects/grades need stronger teachers
  • Faculty ratings — student feedback aggregated per teacher

Approving fee waivers

Go to Fees & Finance → Waiver Requests. Review the parent's request, supporting documents (income certificate, etc.), and approve/decline with a note.

FOR PARENTS

Parent Guide

Stay connected to your child's school day — attendance, grades, fees, bus location, and direct teacher chat. All in one place.

Tracking your child's bus (most-used feature)

  1. Tap the Bus tab at the bottom.
  2. You'll see a live map with your child's bus marked as a gold dot.
  3. The top banner shows: ETA, distance remaining, current speed, and current stop.
  4. When the bus arrives at your child's stop and they board, you'll get an instant notification: "Aarav has boarded Bus B-01".
  5. You'll also get a notification when the bus reaches school.
💡
Tip: Enable "Always" location notifications in your phone settings so you get alerts even when the app isn't open.

Paying fees online

  1. Tap the Fees tab.
  2. You'll see all terms — paid ones marked green ✓, pending ones marked red.
  3. Tap Pay Now on any pending term.
  4. Choose payment method: UPI, Debit/Credit Card, or NetBanking.
  5. You'll get an instant receipt via SMS and in-app. The school is notified immediately.

Messaging your child's teacher

  1. Tap the Messages tab.
  2. You'll see all your child's teachers in the list.
  3. Tap a teacher's name to open the chat.
  4. Type your message. Attach photos or documents if needed.
  5. Teachers usually reply within school hours (9 AM – 4 PM). Don't expect responses on weekends.

Setting up multiple children

If you have more than one child at the school, tap your profile photo → Add Child. Enter the second child's roll number and verify with OTP. You can switch between children using the dropdown at the top of the dashboard.

FOR BUS DRIVERS

Bus Driver Guide

The driver app is intentionally the simplest in Scholaria — one big button to start, a list of stops, and a checklist for students. That's it.

Your daily routine

1
Before starting your route
  • Charge your phone to at least 60%
  • Open Scholaria, select Bus Driver
  • You'll land on the My Route screen showing your route name and stops
2
Starting the route
  • Tap the big gold "Start Route" button
  • When asked, tap "Allow" for location access
  • The banner will turn dark with a pulsing green dot — that means you're broadcasting
3
At each stop
  • Stop the bus safely. Don't operate the app while driving.
  • The current stop card expands automatically
  • As each student boards, tap their name in the list — it turns green ✓
  • Parents of boarded students get notified instantly
4
Moving to the next stop
  • Once all students at the current stop have boarded, tap "Move to Next Stop"
  • The progress bar advances. Drive to the next stop.
5
Arriving at school
  • When you reach the school, a green banner appears: "Arrived at School!"
  • Tap the red "End Route" button
  • Confirm by tapping End Route in the dialog
⚠️ Safety first: Never tap or look at the phone while the bus is moving. Always stop safely before marking students. The app is designed to be operated only at stops.

What if I forget to start the route?

Parents will see your bus as "Offline". They may call the school. Open the app, hit Start Route, and you're back online — past data won't be recovered but live tracking resumes immediately.

What if my phone runs out of battery?

Tracking stops. The bus is marked offline. Plug into a charger and reopen the app — broadcasting resumes. We recommend always carrying a power bank.

Troubleshooting

I'm not receiving notifications

Go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Scholaria → Notifications and ensure all categories are enabled. Also check that your phone isn't on Silent or Do Not Disturb mode.

The bus tracking shows the wrong location

This usually means the driver's phone has weak GPS signal (tunnels, basements, dense buildings). Wait 30 seconds — it should self-correct. If it's stuck for more than 5 minutes, contact the school office.

I can't log in — "Invalid credentials"

First, check you've selected the correct role (Student vs. Parent confusion is common). If still failing, tap "Forgot password" on the login screen to receive a reset link via SMS.

The app is slow / crashing

Try these in order: 1) Close and reopen the app. 2) Restart your phone. 3) Update the app from your app store. 4) If still issues, uninstall and reinstall — your data is safe on our servers.

Payment failed but money was deducted

Don't panic. Failed transactions auto-refund within 5–7 business days. Check your bank statement. If not refunded by day 8, contact us at hello@scholaria.app with the transaction ID.

I want to delete my account

Tap your profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account. You'll have a 30-day grace period during which you can reactivate. After 30 days, all your data is permanently erased.
